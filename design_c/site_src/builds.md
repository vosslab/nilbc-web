# Member creations

Trains are the spine, not the whole hobby. Members build town, space, city, castle, and other stories, and characters wander from one area into the next. Someone who builds mechs, mosaics, or spacecraft can still take part. Shared modules use club standards so neighboring builds fit together.

## If you connect track

Before connecting to the shared layout, [contact the club](join.md) for current track,
train, and skyscraper specifications. Members supply track for two main lines across
their tables. Builders who are not connecting to the main lines are welcome too.
