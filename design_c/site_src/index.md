# <span class="name-place">Northern Illinois</span> <span class="name-club">Brick Club</span>

Adult builders across northern Illinois and southern Wisconsin. The club is changing its name from Northern Illinois LEGO Train Club (NILTC) to Northern Illinois Brick Club (NILBC). Trains still run through the display. Builders who never put a train on the table are welcome.

<p class="note">The club's current public site still uses the NILTC name.</p>

### Visit a show

Most library displays are free to walk through. Some hosts hand out a free timed ticket at the door. The trains run, and the tables around them hold towns, stories, and plenty of other builds.
