## What a visit is like

You are looking at a large temporary layout, not a store display. Battery-powered trains run on two shared lines. Around those lines are towns, castles, spacecraft, vehicles, and small scenes. The club's 13 October 2026 Facebook post for Northbrook described monsters, robots, dragons, princesses, planes, spaceships, superheroes, and Halloween builds along with the trains, and said that weekend was free.

Northbrook Public Library's own fall guide matched the club hours for 17-18 October 2026 and placed the display in the Pollak Room: Saturday 9:00-17:00 and Sunday 13:00-17:00. That show is over. Some other hosts, including a past Palatine weekend, used a free timed ticket at the door. Read the host's page for the weekend you plan to attend.
