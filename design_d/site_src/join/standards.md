# Build standards

Shared standards let one member's module line up with everyone else's on show day. Here is
the short version. The full specifications, with diagrams, are on the
[club's standards pages](https://niltc.org/standards/); use them before you build.

/// cards

### :material-train-variant: Track and trains

Track placement, clearances, and curve sizes (R104 and R120 curves) keep every train moving
across every module. Train standards set limits on width, length, and height.

:material-arrow-right: [Track specifications](https://niltc.org/standards/track-specifications/) and
[train specifications](https://niltc.org/standards/train-specifications/).

### :material-office-building-outline: Skyscraper city

The collaborative skyscraper display is built on city blocks of 64 by 64 or 96 by 96 studs,
joined by shared street sections.

:material-arrow-right: [Skyscraper standards](https://niltc.org/standards/skyscrapers/).

### :material-castle: Castle

The castle display, started in 2020, uses 15 by 60 inch modules. Minifigures are classic
yellow.

:material-arrow-right: [Castle standards](https://niltc.org/castle/).

### :material-table-furniture: Tables and skirting

Display tables, clear barriers, and skirting follow shared sizes so the layout looks like
one display. The specifications include plans for building your own table.

:material-arrow-right: [Table standards](https://niltc.org/standards/tables/).

## Themes

The theme rule is short: builds must be family friendly and not overtly offensive. Inside
that, build what you love.
