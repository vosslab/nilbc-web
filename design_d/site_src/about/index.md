# About the club

Northern Illinois Brick Club is an independent, self-sustaining club of adult brick
builders. We give members a place to explore the hobby together and share their creations
with the public.

## A new name for a bigger table

Since 2001 we have been the **Northern Illinois LEGO&reg; Train Club (NILTC)**. Our new
name, **Northern Illinois Brick Club (NILBC)**, does two things:

- **It removes a trademark from our name.** LEGO is a trademark of the LEGO Group. The club
  is not sponsored by the LEGO Group, and its name no longer suggests that it is.
- **It says who we already are.** Members have always built far more than trains: town,
  space, city, castle, Star Wars, Harry Potter, and more. A builder who has never laid
  track should know right away that there is a seat for them.

The trains are not going anywhere. They still run through almost every layout we bring to
a show.

The club also introduced a new identity: a round logo, a banner, and a mascot,
[Matchstick](matchstick.md), a brick-built dragon who travels between every world members
create.

While the change rolls out, you will still see the NILTC name on our email address and
social media.

## Our story so far

/// steps | timeline

1. **2001: Started**
   The club began in late summer 2001.
2. **2002: First show**
   Five members built a 17 by 22 foot display for the NMRA High Wheeler show at Harper
   College in Palatine. Later that year the club was invited to the Train Collectors
   Association national show in St. Charles.
3. **2003: Biggest crowd**
   A 30 by 30 foot display at the Illinois Railway Museum in Union drew about 48,000 guests
   over the event.
4. **to 2022: A holiday tradition**
   A Christmas train show at Cantigny Park in Wheaton ran for twenty annual shows. In 2011
   it drew more than 10,800 visitors.
5. **Today: On the road**
   Around nine shows a year at public libraries, gardens, Brickworld Chicago each June, and
   Nebraska Brick Days.

## Who we are

- **Adults.** Members are 18 or older. Our public shows are made for all ages.
- **Spread out.** Members live across northern Illinois and southern Wisconsin. There is no
  permanent layout; we build it fresh for each show.
- **Independent.** Displays belong to members or were bought by the club. The LEGO Group
  does not sponsor us.
- **Recognized.** The club is listed on the LEGO Ambassador Network, the LEGO Group's program
  for recognized adult fan clubs.
