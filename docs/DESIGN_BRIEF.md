# NILBC website brief

## Your assignment

you are making a demo site for the club to consider your design. You own this entire repository. 
Create the strongest Northern Illinois Brick Club website you can using MkDocs Material, with static 
output compatible with GitHub Pages. You may modify any file, reorganize the project, introduce 
dependencies, and make your own technical and visual decisions. The starter is a working starting 
point, not a required architecture. These permissions include inherited scaffolding and documentation.

Develop a distinctive, polished website. Make your own decisions about composition,
typography, color treatment, photography, illustration, navigation, interactions, and page
structure. Choose how prominently Matchstick appears. Preserve the approved club identity
and the mascot's recognizable brick-built character.

## Club and audience

The club is changing its name from NILTC (Northern Illinois LEGO Train Club) to NILBC
(Northern Illinois Brick Club). The new name removes the LEGO trademark from the club name
and welcomes builders with many interests. The club still builds and exhibits trains.

Serve prospective adult members and members of the public, including families visiting shows.
Help visitors understand the club, explore member creations, find exhibition information,
and discover how to join. Make non-train builders feel welcome.

The [existing club website](https://niltc.org/) is a source for club information and public
club photographs. Verify time-sensitive facts before publishing them. Label unresolved
information clearly; use real club work when presenting member creations. Record sources
and credits for any additional assets.

## Approved identity

Read [ASSETS.md](ASSETS.md) and inspect the supplied visual references.
Matchstick is a physical, brick-built dragon mascot, with visible construction details,
oversized expressive eyes, compact proportions, and a curled tail. Preserve that connection
when adapting the character. An unrelated generic dragon does not represent Matchstick.

The working mascot palette is Red `#C91A09`, Tan `#E4CD9E`, Dark Azure `#078BC9`,
and Medium Lavender `#AC78BA`, with white eyes and near-black pupils. This is a provisional
digital reference, pending confirmation from the actual parts list. It does not prescribe
the overall website palette.

## Quality and delivery

- Make the website work well on phones and larger screens.
- Support keyboard use, visible focus, readable contrast, useful alternative text,
  and reduced-motion preferences.
- Deliver complete editable source, declared dependencies, and accurate setup/build instructions.
- Verify the built website, its links, assets, and important visitor journeys.
- Include representative screenshots, checks actually run, asset credits, and known limitations.
- Keep dated changes and settled decisions in the repository's documentation.

Use your judgment to make the site excellent. There is no required layout, visual direction,
directory structure, CSS system, template organization, or page count.
