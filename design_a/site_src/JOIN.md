# Join the club

Your next build belongs here.

You do not have to build trains to find your people here. Northern Illinois Brick Club
welcomes adult builders with a wide range of interests, while keeping our train-building roots.

More interests. More possibilities.

---

## Start with a conversation

These three steps can help you make an introduction. They are not an application process;
the club can confirm its current membership arrangements.

1. **Bring your interests**

    Buildings, vehicles, fantasy, landscapes, trains, or something entirely your own.
    Think about what you enjoy making and what you would like to share. This is a starting point,
    not a portfolio requirement.

2. **Find the club's public channels**

    Visit the existing club website or its linked Facebook page. Introduce yourself as an adult
    builder interested in learning more about Northern Illinois Brick Club.

3. **Ask about getting involved**

    Ask about meeting arrangements, dues, the current membership process, and how members
    participate in displays and activities. Confirm those details directly before making plans.
