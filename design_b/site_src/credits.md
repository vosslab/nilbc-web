# Credits and sources

This is a design demonstration for Northern Illinois Brick Club, prepared October 19, 2026.

## Club photographs

Photographs show the club's builds and exhibitions.

## Club identity

The NILBC banner and circular Matchstick logo are owner-supplied approved artwork.
The mascot artwork depicts a brick-built model; it is not a photograph of a physical dragon model.
Artist credit and a source parts list were not supplied. Original artwork is preserved.
