# Upcoming shows

Come see the layouts in person. Our shows are hosted by public libraries and partner venues
around the Chicago suburbs. Members bring trains, cities, and other builds to share.

Each host venue sets its own hours, parking, and policies, so check the venue's website
before you travel.

## Invite us to your venue

We exhibit at libraries, museums, conventions, and holiday events. If you run a venue in
northern Illinois and want a brick display, email
[niltc.contact@gmail.com](mailto:niltc.contact@gmail.com?subject=Exhibit%20invitation).
