# Join the club

We welcome adult builders with all kinds of interests. Tell us what you like to build
and what you would like to do with the club.

/// split

## What members do

- **Exhibit.** Members bring their builds to about nine public shows a year and help set up
  shared layouts.
- **Build together.** Collaborative displays, such as the skyscraper city and the castle,
  combine modules from many members built to shared standards.
- **Meet.** The club meets about once a quarter, at members' homes around the western
  suburbs of Chicago.
- **Talk shop.** Techniques, lighting, train power, parts sourcing, and what to build next.

## How to join

Email [niltc.contact@gmail.com](mailto:niltc.contact@gmail.com?subject=Membership) and include:

- Your name
- Your age
- Where you live
- What you like to build
- Projects you are working on
- Links to photos or videos of your builds

The club will invite you to a meeting to meet members and see what the club is like.

## Not a train builder?

You are welcome here. Trains remain part of our displays, but the
only theme rule for club displays is that builds are family friendly. Members build town,
space, city, castle, Star Wars, Harry Potter, and more. Collaborative displays have their
own [build standards](standards.md) so modules from different members line up.

## Questions

??? question "Does the LEGO Group sponsor the club?"
    No. Our displays are privately owned by members or purchased by the club. The club is
    independent and self-sustaining.

??? question "Is there a permanent layout I can visit?"
    No. Members live across northern Illinois and southern Wisconsin, and the club builds its
    layouts fresh for each [show](../shows/index.md).

??? question "Do I need to own a lot of bricks to join?"
    Tell us what you build and how much you have when you write. The club asks for photos
    or videos of your work so members can get to know your building.

??? question "Why does the email still say NILTC?"
    The club is moving from Northern Illinois LEGO Train Club (NILTC) to Northern Illinois
    Brick Club (NILBC). Email and social accounts keep the old name until they move over.
